# Rollout and adoption

Most automations fail on adoption, not on technology. People keep the old spreadsheet open "just in case", and the savings never arrive. This skill plans the switch.

## Steps

1. **Pilot.** One team or region, two to four weeks, with named users. Define what the pilot must show to go wider: pass rate in real use, time per case, number of fallbacks to the manual route.

2. **Training.** Short and task-based: one session per role on the tasks that role does, recorded, plus a one-page guide with screenshots. Name who answers questions in the first weeks and how users reach them.

3. **Owner after go-live.** One named person who decides on changes, watches the measures and reviews reported wrong outputs. Name a backup.

4. **Switch-off.** The date the old route stops: the shared folder becomes read-only, the old form is removed, the old report is no longer sent. Without a date, two routes run in parallel and double the work.

5. **Adoption tracking.** Weekly for the first two months:
   - share of cases going through the new route
   - fallbacks to the manual route and why
   - wrong outputs reported and fixed
   - questions to support, by theme

## Output

`rollout-plan.md` with the five sections above and a dated calendar.

## Rules

- Do not switch off the old route before the pilot criteria are met.
- Treat every fallback as information: it points to an input, a rule or a training gap.
